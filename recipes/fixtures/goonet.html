NISSAN SILVIA Description

The "Silvia," a car with a lightweight body of small passenger car size, was released by Nissan. The first model appeared in 1965, and only this model was manufactured at the then Tonouchi Manufacturing plant. Body types available for this car included coupe, convertible, and hatchback. The 7th generation model, released in January 1999, was developed under the concept of "A nimble, compact, sporty coupe that evokes emotion through seeing, riding, and driving," and its body size, which had been enlarged in the previous model, was returned to the 5-number vehicle frame. Production ended in August 2002 due to factors such as exhaust gas regulations. Grades for the model released in January 2002 included the "Spec S V Package" and "Spec R V Package." In terms of safety, this model came standard with ABS, dual airbags, and brake assist. Body colors such as "Sparkling Silver" and "Brilliant Blue" were available. The information in this description is current as of October 2018.
